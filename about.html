[The Origin Story]

How It All Started

[01]

Origin Story

It Started With a Disaster

Most town planners start with a degree in geography. Richard Kemp started with a disaster.

As a teenager, Richard became obsessed with the original SimCity. Perfect city design. Balanced budget. Everything in order. Then the UFOs arrived.

Random disasters would drop from the sky and stall your progress. No warning. No logic. Just chaos.

Richard realised early that in the real world, the Council is the UFO. Perfect design and a ready budget mean nothing if you cannot navigate the regulatory obstacles in front of you.

That realisation shaped everything that came after.

[02]

The Method

To Beat the System, You Have to Learn It From the Inside

After graduating at the top of his class from the University of Auckland, Richard went into the belly of the beast: QLDC.

Working as a Resource Consenting Planner across both the Wanaka and Queenstown offices, Richard did not just read the rules. He saw how they were interpreted. He sat in the meetings where applications were picked apart. He learned the specific language, the unwritten preferences, and the bureaucratic triggers that reliably lead to a No.

That is knowledge you cannot get from a textbook. You have to earn it from the inside.

[03]

Why Pragmatic Planning Exists

Developers and Homeowners Needed Someone Who Spoke Council as a First Language

Richard saw a clear gap. Property owners across the Wakatipu Basin were hiring planners who understood the rules in theory. What they needed was someone who understood how the rules were actually applied.

He founded Pragmatic Planning with a single mission: provide the shortest path to Granted.

Since 2015, Richard has navigated 500+ projects through the QLDC and Central Government frameworks. He has seen the full transition from the old RMA to the 2026 Planning Bill, maintaining a near-perfect approval rate by staying three steps ahead of policy shifts.
