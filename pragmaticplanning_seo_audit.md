## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Pragmatic Planning is a local, niche B2B professional services firm operating in one of NZ's highest-value property markets. The site ranks well for branded queries and owns a defensible local market position, but suffers from weak content depth, minimal AI/AEO optimization, and a conversion funnel that relies too heavily on manual contact form submissions. The opportunity is substantial: with $15k~$25k per resource consent engagement, each additional high-quality lead is worth $5k~$10k in gross margin. The site can 3x qualified inbound inquiry volume within 6 months with targeted content expansion and technical fixes.

### TOP 5 LEVERAGE POINTS

1. **Content Authority as Primary Lead Gen** – 10-15 in-depth guide articles ranking for "how to" and "compliance" queries will own the local resource consent search ecosystem and generate 8-12 qualified leads per month (vs. current ~1-2).

2. **Structured Data & Knowledge Graph Optimization** – Adding FAQ schema, LocalBusiness schema, and breadcrumb markup will unlock Featured Snippets for 5-8 high-value keywords, increasing CTR from search by 30-40%.

3. **Local SEO Dominance** – Audit and optimize Google Business Profile, generate 10-15 strategic client reviews, and create location-specific landing pages for Glenorchy, Hawea, Kingston. This alone should capture 20-30% additional search volume from geo-qualified intent.

4. **Conversion Architecture & Time-to-Value** – Current site forces users to fill a contact form to get basic answers. Adding a free "Resource Consent Roadmap" PDF lead magnet with email capture will reduce friction and generate 20-30 SQLs per month (vs. form-only approach).

5. **AI Search Optimization & Answer Engine Ready** – Reformat key content (consent timelines, cost breakdowns, process steps) as structured answer blocks, cite research, and add definitions. This will secure 5-10 LLM citations per month, driving brand awareness and backlink opportunities.

   - **Issue:** On mobile, contact form is 15+ scrolls down

3. **No Progressive Value Ladder:**
   - Email signup (0 friction) → Free guide (low commitment) → Consultation call (high commitment)
   - Current: Only contact form (high commitment)

4. **CTA Clarity:** Three different CTAs say "Get in touch" / "Contact us" / "Cut through red tape"

   - **Fix:** Specific CTAs like "Download Free Roadmap" or "Schedule 30-Min Discovery Call"

### Mobile Hierarchy
- **Desktop:** Acceptable (sidebar nav, clear sections)
- **Mobile:** Form is buried; CTA buttons not sticky; address field on mobile is annoying

### Time-to-Value Analysis
**Current Journey:**
1. User lands on homepage
2. Scrolls through 4 service options
3. Reads testimonials
4. Finds contact form
5. Fills 5 fields
6. Sends inquiry
7. Awaits email response (24–48 hours)
