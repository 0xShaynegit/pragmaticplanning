How We Can Help

We provide practical planning advice and resource consent services for property owners, developers and businesses across Queenstown Lakes.

01

Resource Consents

Whether you're building a home, undertaking alterations, or pursuing a larger development, we manage the resource consent process from start to finish. We provide practical advice, prepare robust applications, and work closely with Council to help achieve successful outcomes.

02

Airbnb & Short-Term Accommodation

Queenstown Lakes' short-term accommodation rules can be complex. We specialise in helping property owners obtain resource consent for visitor accommodation, providing clear advice and managing the process from initial assessment through to approval.

03

Subdivisions

From simple two-lot subdivisions to larger development projects, we provide strategic planning advice and coordinate the resource consent process through to approval. We work closely with surveyors, engineers and Council to help achieve successful outcomes.

04

Planning Advice

Good planning advice can save significant time, cost and risk. Whether you're assessing a property's development potential, undertaking due diligence, or exploring project options, we provide clear, practical advice to help you make informed decisions.
