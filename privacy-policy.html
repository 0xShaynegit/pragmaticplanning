Privacy Policy

Introduction

Pragmatic Planning (referred to as "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 2020 (New Zealand). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you interact with our website (pragmaticplanning.co.nz) or engage our services.

By using our website or engaging our services, you agree to the collection and use of personal information in accordance with this policy.

What Personal Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of personal information:

  • Contact information: name, email address, phone number, and postal address, provided when you make an enquiry or engage our services.
  • Property information: property address, title details, and planning-related information you provide in connection with a service enquiry or engagement.
  • Communications: records of correspondence with us by email, phone, or through our website contact form.
  • Website usage data: anonymised analytics data including pages visited, time on site, and referring sources, collected via Cloudflare Web Analytics. This data does not identify individuals and does not use cookies.

We do not collect sensitive personal information (as defined under the Privacy Act 2020) unless specifically required to provide our services and with your explicit consent.

How We Use Your Personal Information

We use personal information collected for the following purposes:

  • To respond to enquiries and provide the planning and consultancy services you have requested.
  • To prepare and submit resource consent applications, planning reports, or other professional documentation on your behalf.
  • To communicate with you about the progress of your matter.
  • To comply with our legal and professional obligations under the Resource Management Act 1991, Building Act 2004, and other applicable New Zealand legislation.
  • To improve our website and services using anonymised, aggregated analytics data.

We will not use your personal information for any purpose other than those described above without your prior consent.

Disclosure of Personal Information

We may disclose your personal information to third parties only in the following circumstances:

  • Queenstown Lakes District Council (QLDC) and other regulatory bodies: as required to submit resource consent applications, building consent applications, or other planning-related documents on your behalf.
  • Other professionals engaged on your matter: surveyors, engineers, lawyers, or other specialists with whom we collaborate to provide our services, with your knowledge and agreement.
  • Legal requirements: where we are required by law to disclose information to a government agency, court, or other authority.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing or commercial purposes.

Retention of Personal Information

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and to meet our legal and professional obligations. Records relating to resource consent applications and professional planning engagements may be retained for up to seven years following the completion of a matter, in accordance with standard professional practice and applicable statutory limitation periods.

Security of Personal Information

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, use, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Personal information is stored on secure systems and access is limited to those with a legitimate need to use it in connection with our services.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights Under the Privacy Act 2020

Under the Privacy Act 2020, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of any personal information that is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, or misleading.
  • Complain to us if you believe we have breached the Privacy Act 2020, and to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner if your complaint is not resolved to your satisfaction.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@pragmaticplanning.co.nz. We will respond to your request within 20 working days, as required by the Privacy Act 2020.

Links to Other Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites (for example, QLDC's Edocs portal or mapping viewer). These links are provided for your convenience and information. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites. We recommend you review the privacy policies of any external websites you visit.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. The most current version will always be available on this page. We will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy when changes are made.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your personal information, please contact us:

  • Email: info@pragmaticplanning.co.nz
  • Phone: 021 104 3405
  • Postal Address: Pragmatic Planning, Queenstown Lakes District, New Zealand

If you are not satisfied with our response to a privacy complaint, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at www.privacy.org.nz.
