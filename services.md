# Services   Pragmatic Planning

## Resource Consents

Resource consent management for property owners, developers, and businesses in Queenstown Lakes District.

**What is included:**
- Initial site and District Plan assessment
- Pre-application meeting with QLDC (where appropriate)
- Preparation of resource consent application and supporting documents
- Coordination with surveyors, engineers, and specialists
- Response to Requests for Further Information (RFI) from Council
- Attendance at hearings (limited and fully notified applications)
- Post-decision support through to building consent stage

**Typical timeframe:** Non-notified: 20 working days from acceptance. Limited notified: 4 to 6 months. Fully notified: 6 to 12+ months.

**Pricing:** Fee estimate provided at initial consultation. QLDC charges a processing deposit on lodgement plus hourly rates to decision. No fixed price due to variable complexity.

## Airbnb and Short-Term Accommodation

Activity status advice and resource consent for visitor accommodation under the QLDC Operative and Proposed District Plan.

**What is included:**
- Activity status assessment (permitted, controlled, discretionary)
- Homestay vs visitor accommodation pathway advice
- Resource consent application preparation
- Management plan preparation
- Neighbour affected party strategy

**Who this is for:** Property owners wishing to let via Airbnb, Bookabach, or similar platforms in Queenstown Lakes residential zones.

**Key rule:** In most QLDC residential zones, short-term letting of an unhosted property requires resource consent regardless of the number of nights.

## Subdivisions

Subdivision consent from feasibility assessment through to new titles.

**What is included:**
- Feasibility and zoning assessment
- Development Contributions estimate
- Subdivision consent application
- Coordination with licensed cadastral surveyor and civil engineer
- Section 224(c) sign-off coordination
- LINZ lodgement support

**Typical timeframe:** Standard two to three lot subdivision: 12 to 18 months from initial advice to new titles.

**Development Contributions (2026 estimate):** $25,000 to $45,000 per additional equivalent residential unit in Queenstown catchment. Wanaka rates differ.

## Planning Advice

Desktop and site-specific planning advice for property purchases, development feasibility, and problem resolution.

**What is included:**
- Pre-purchase due diligence (desktop assessment of development potential)
- Section 37 Building Act advice and resolution
- Hazard register and natural hazard assessment
- Granny flat and minor dwelling feasibility
- Retrospective consent advice
- National Policy Statement assessment

**Pricing:** Charged at hourly rate. Initial free, no-obligation conversation available.
